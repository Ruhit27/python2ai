description: Anyone affected by, or with influence over, a business or a decision: owners, staff, customers, suppliers, and more.
---

Any person or group affected by a business or a decision, or able to influence it. That includes [shareholders](./Shareholder.md), employees, customers, suppliers, lenders, regulators, and the local community.

Inside a company, the word is used more narrowly for the people with a say or an interest in a specific project. For a new pricing page, stakeholders might include marketing, sales, finance, and legal. Identifying stakeholders early — and knowing who decides versus who needs to be informed — avoids surprise objections late in a project.

Stakeholders' interests often conflict. Shareholders may want higher [profit](./Profit.md), employees higher pay, and customers lower [prices](./Pricing.md). Much of management is balancing those interests. In [B2B](./B2B%20and%20B2C.md) sales, winning a deal usually means convincing several stakeholders at the customer's company, not just one.

_Avoid:_ "shareholder" — a shareholder is one specific kind of stakeholder.

_Usage:_

"Legal just blocked the launch. Why weren't they involved?"

"We missed them as a stakeholder. Let's map everyone who needs to sign off before the next one."
