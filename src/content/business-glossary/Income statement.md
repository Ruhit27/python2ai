description: The report that shows revenue, costs, and profit over a period. Also called the profit and loss (P&L).
---

A financial report that shows how much a business earned and spent over a period — a month, a quarter, a year — and whether it made a [profit](./Profit.md). It reads top to bottom: [revenue](./Revenue.md) first, then [costs](./Cost.md) taken away in stages, ending with net profit.

A simplified one for a coffee shop:

| Line | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $48,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | −$12,000 |
| Gross profit | $36,000 |
| Operating expenses | −$30,000 |
| Net profit | $6,000 |

It is one of three core financial statements, alongside the [balance sheet](./Balance%20sheet.md) (what the company owns and owes at one moment) and the [cash flow statement](./Cash%20flow%20statement.md) (where the cash actually went). The income statement uses [accrual](./Accrual.md) accounting, so it shows what was earned, not what was paid.

_Avoid:_ "P&L" and "profit and loss statement" are common names for the same thing, used especially in the UK and in internal reports.

_Usage:_

"Where do I see if last quarter was profitable?"

"The income statement — bottom line, net profit."
