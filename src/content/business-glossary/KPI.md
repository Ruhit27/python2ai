description: Key performance indicator: one of the few numbers a team watches to judge whether it is succeeding.
---

Stands for **K**ey **P**erformance **I**ndicator: a measurable number chosen to show whether a business, team, or process is doing well. "Key" is the important word — a KPI is one of a few numbers that really matter, not every metric that can be tracked.

Good KPIs follow directly from goals. A subscription business might track monthly [revenue](./Revenue.md), [churn](./Churn.md), and [CAC](./CAC.md). A support team might track average response time and customer satisfaction. A warehouse might track [lead time](./Lead%20time.md) and order accuracy.

KPIs are often used with [OKRs](./OKR.md): the KPI is the number watched continuously, while the OKR sets a specific target to move it this quarter. A common trap is that when a number becomes a target, people find ways to hit it without improving what it measures — a support team judged only on response speed might answer quickly but badly.

_Avoid:_ "metric" as a synonym — every KPI is a metric, but most metrics are not key.

_Usage:_

"We track 40 metrics on the dashboard."

"Which three are the KPIs? If everything's key, nothing is."
