description: What people think and feel about a company, beyond the product itself: its reputation and identity.
---

What people think and feel when they hear a company's name: its reputation, personality, and promise. A brand includes the visible parts — name, logo, colors, tone of voice — but it's mostly made of experiences: how the product works, how support treats you, what other people say.

A strong brand is valuable because it lowers the need to persuade. Customers who already trust a company convert faster, pay more, and come back, which lowers [CAC](./CAC.md), supports higher [prices](./Pricing.md), and reduces [churn](./Churn.md). That's why a trusted brand is a common [competitive advantage](./Competitive%20advantage.md): a rival can copy the product but not years of reputation.

A brand expresses the [value proposition](./Value%20proposition.md) — ideally the two tell the same story. Brand matters most in [B2C](./B2B%20and%20B2C.md), but in B2B it matters too: many buyers prefer a known vendor because choosing it is safe.

_Avoid:_ "logo" — the logo is a small visible part of a brand, not the brand.

_Usage:_

"Why can they charge twice as much for basically the same product?"

"Brand. People trust them to work, and they don't want to take a risk on an unknown."
