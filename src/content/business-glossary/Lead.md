description: A potential customer who has shown some interest, such as signing up or making contact, but hasn't bought yet.
---

A person or company that might become a customer and has shown some sign of interest — filling in a form, downloading a guide, booking a demo, replying to an email — but hasn't bought yet.

Leads enter at the top of the [funnel](./Funnel.md) and are sorted by how likely they are to buy. A lead that fits the target customer and has shown clear buying intent is often called *qualified*. In [B2B](./B2B%20and%20B2C.md) sales, marketing typically generates leads and hands the most promising ones to the sales team.

The number of leads matters less than their quality. Ten well-matched leads can bring in more [revenue](./Revenue.md) than a thousand random email sign-ups. Tracking where leads come from, and their [conversion rate](./Conversion%20rate.md) into customers, shows which marketing is worth its cost — a key input to [CAC](./CAC.md).

_Avoid:_ "customer" or "prospect" loosely — a prospect usually means a lead that has been qualified as a real possible buyer; a customer has paid.

_Usage:_

"Marketing brought in 2,000 leads this month!"

"And how many became customers? Last month it was 12. Let's check the quality before we celebrate."
