description: The manager accountable for a business unit's revenue, costs, and profit — its own profit and loss.
---

The person responsible for the financial results of a business unit, product line, or region — its revenue, its costs, and the resulting [profit](./Profit.md). The name comes from *P&L*, short for profit and loss, the common name for the [income statement](./Income%20statement.md).

Being a P&L owner means more than managing spending. A manager who controls only a [budget](./Budget.md) is judged on staying within it; a P&L owner is judged on the whole result, so they can decide to spend more — more [headcount](./Headcount.md), more marketing — if they expect it to bring in more [revenue](./Revenue.md). That authority to trade off revenue against costs is what defines the role.

Large companies split themselves into many P&Ls so each leader has clear ownership and can be measured fairly. It's also a common step in a career path: running a P&L shows you can think like a business owner, not just a department head.

_Avoid:_ "budget holder" — a budget holder manages costs; a P&L owner answers for revenue and profit too.

_Usage:_

"Who decides whether we open a second office in the north?"

"The regional manager — they own the P&L for the north, so the cost and the revenue land on her numbers."
