Short for *cardiovascular exercise*: activity that keeps your heart rate raised for a sustained period — running, cycling, swimming, rowing, brisk walking. It trains the heart, lungs, and blood vessels, and improves endurance and [VO2 max](./VO2%20max.md).

Cardio comes in different intensities. Easy, steady efforts are [LISS](./LISS.md) or [Zone 2](./Zone%202.md) training; short, very hard bursts are [HIIT](./HIIT.md). [Heart rate zones](./Heart%20rate%20zones.md) are one way of describing where an effort sits.

Cardio and lifting work well together. The fear that cardio "kills gains" is mostly unfounded: moderate amounts have little effect on [muscle growth](./Muscle%20hypertrophy.md), especially if it's cycling or walking rather than lots of running, and it's kept away from leg sessions. Cardio may also help [recovery](./Recovery.md) between sessions, and it burns extra calories during a [cut](./Cutting.md).

_Avoid:_ "cardio kills gains" — sensible amounts barely affect muscle growth, and they improve health a lot.

_Usage:_

"If I do cardio, will I lose muscle?"

"Not with two or three sessions a week. Just avoid a hard run right before leg day."
