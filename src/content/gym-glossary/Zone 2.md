---
description: Steady cardio at an easy-to-moderate effort — you can talk but not sing. Builds your aerobic base.
---

A level of [cardio](./Cardio.md) effort in the second of five [heart rate zones](./Heart%20rate%20zones.md) — roughly 60–70% of your maximum heart rate. It feels easy to moderate: you can speak in full sentences, but you wouldn't want to sing.

Zone 2 training builds the *aerobic base*: it improves the heart's capacity and the muscles' ability to use fat and oxygen for fuel. Endurance athletes do most of their training in this zone, and it's become popular for general health because it builds fitness while leaving plenty of energy for other training, like lifting.

It's very similar to [LISS](./LISS.md), just defined more precisely. A better aerobic base may also help [recovery](./Recovery.md) between hard sets and sessions, and it supports gains in [VO2 max](./VO2%20max.md), though harder work like [HIIT](./HIIT.md) improves VO2 max more directly.

_Avoid:_ "fat-burning zone" — you do burn more fat as a share of fuel at easy effort, but total calories burned is what matters for fat loss.

_Usage:_

"How do I know I'm in Zone 2?"

"If you can talk in full sentences but it's clearly not a stroll, you're about there."
