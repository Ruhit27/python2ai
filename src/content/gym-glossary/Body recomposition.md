description: Losing fat and building muscle at the same time. Most realistic for beginners and people returning to training.
---

Changing your body's make-up by losing fat and gaining muscle at the same time, so your shape changes even if your bodyweight barely moves. Often shortened to *recomp*.

For a long time it was thought you had to choose between [bulking](./Bulking.md) (gaining) and [cutting](./Cutting.md) (losing). Research shows recomposition is realistic in some situations: for **beginners**, for people **returning after a break** (muscle regrows faster the second time), and for people with more body fat to lose. Experienced, lean lifters recomp very slowly, if at all.

From the training side, recomp requires the same things as muscle growth: hard, progressive training ([progressive overload](./Progressive%20overload.md)) and enough [volume](./Volume.md). Eating plenty of [protein](./Protein.md) and roughly at or slightly below maintenance calories supports it. Because weight stays similar, progress is best tracked with photos, measurements, and [PRs](./PR.md) rather than the scale.

_Avoid:_ "toning up" — recomposition is the real process people usually mean by it.

_Usage:_

"The scale hasn't moved in two months, but my jeans are looser."

"That's recomposition — you're losing fat and gaining muscle at the same time."
