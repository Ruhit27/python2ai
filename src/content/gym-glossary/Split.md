---
description: How a training week is divided up — which muscles or movements are trained on which days.
---

How you divide your training across the week. A **full-body** split trains everything each session. An **upper/lower** split alternates upper-body days and leg days. **Push/pull/legs** separates pushing muscles, pulling muscles, and legs. A **bro split** gives each muscle group its own day.

The split decides your [training frequency](./Training%20frequency.md) — how often each muscle gets trained — and how the weekly [volume](./Volume.md) is spread out. Research suggests that for most people, training each muscle about twice a week works slightly better than once, as long as total volume is the same.

The right split depends mostly on your schedule. Three days a week suits a [full-body](./Full-body%20workout.md) split; four suits upper/lower; five or six suit [push/pull/legs](./Push%20pull%20legs.md). Beginners usually do best with simpler splits and more frequent practice of the main lifts.

_Avoid:_ "split" as a gymnastic split — in lifting it means how the week is organized.

_Usage:_

"I can only train three days a week. Which split?"

"Full-body. Each muscle still gets trained three times."
