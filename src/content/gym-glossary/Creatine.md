description: A natural compound that helps muscles make quick energy. As a supplement, it reliably improves strength and power in training.
---

A compound made naturally in the body and found in meat and fish, stored mostly in muscles. It helps regenerate the quick energy muscles use for short, hard efforts — like a heavy [set](./Set.md) of a few [reps](./Rep.md) or a sprint.

As a supplement (usually *creatine monohydrate*), it is one of the most researched in sport. Topping up the muscles' stores lets you squeeze out an extra rep or two in hard sets, which over time adds up to more [strength](./Strength.md), more [volume](./Volume.md), and somewhat more [muscle growth](./Muscle%20hypertrophy.md) alongside a good [program](./Program.md). It helps most in lifting and repeated sprints, and little in long [cardio](./Cardio.md).

It commonly causes a quick gain of about 1–2 kg (2–4 lb), which is water stored inside the muscles, not fat. It's not a steroid and not a hormone. The nutrition side — how it's taken and the evidence on safety — is covered in the Nutrition Glossary.

_Avoid:_ "creatine is a steroid" — it's a natural compound found in food, completely unrelated to steroids.

_Usage:_

"I gained a kilo (2 lb) the week I started creatine. Is that fat?"

"No — that's water inside your muscles. Totally normal."
