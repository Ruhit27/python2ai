description: How often you train — either how many sessions a week, or how many times each muscle is trained per week.
---

How often you train. It can mean sessions per week ("I train four times a week"), but in programming it more often means **how many times each muscle is trained per week**.

Research suggests that training each [muscle group](./Muscle%20group.md) about twice a week tends to build a bit more muscle than once a week, for the same total [volume](./Volume.md). Spreading 12 sets across two sessions (6 + 6) lets every set be done fresh, instead of the last sets of one long session being done tired. Beyond twice a week, the benefits flatten out for most people.

Frequency is set by your [split](./Split.md): a [full-body](./Full-body%20workout.md) program trains each muscle every session; a [bro split](./Bro%20split.md) only once a week. Higher frequency also means more practice of each lift, which is why it helps beginners improve [form](./Form.md) and [strength](./Strength.md) quickly.

_Avoid:_ "more frequent is always better" — past about twice per muscle per week, the difference is small.

_Usage:_

"I hit legs once a week, really hard."

"Try splitting those sets across two days. Same volume, better quality."
