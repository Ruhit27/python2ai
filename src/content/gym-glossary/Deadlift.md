description: Lifting a loaded barbell from the floor to standing by driving through the legs and hips.
---

A lift where you pick a loaded barbell up from the floor until you're standing straight, then put it back down. It works nearly the whole back side of the body — glutes, hamstrings, back muscles — plus the grip and core. It's one of the three powerlifting lifts.

There are several versions. The **conventional** deadlift uses a hip-width stance with hands outside the knees. The **sumo** deadlift uses a very wide stance with hands inside the knees. The **Romanian deadlift** (RDL) starts from standing and lowers the bar only to about mid-shin, focusing on the hamstrings.

Because it lets you lift very heavy weights, the deadlift is one of the best exercises for overall [strength](./Strength.md). It's also tiring, so many [programs](./Program.md) use fewer [sets](./Set.md) of it than of other [compound exercises](./Compound%20exercise.md). [Form](./Form.md) matters: a neutral, braced back and the bar kept close to the legs.

_Avoid:_ "deadlifts are bad for your back" — with good form and sensible progression, they strengthen it.

_Usage:_

"Should I deadlift twice a week like I squat?"

"Probably once is enough. Deadlifts are very tiring, so a little goes a long way."
