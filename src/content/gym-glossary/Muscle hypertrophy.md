description: Muscle growth: individual muscle fibers getting bigger through training. What gym slang calls "gains".
---

The growth of muscle, where individual [muscle fibers](./Muscle%20fiber.md) become thicker. It's what people mean by "gains" or "building muscle". Hypertrophy happens when muscles are trained hard enough to signal a need to adapt, then given the materials and rest to rebuild — enough [protein](./Protein.md), calories, and [sleep](./Sleep.md).

The main training drivers are mechanical tension (lifting challenging loads through a good [range of motion](./Range%20of%20motion.md)), enough [volume](./Volume.md) (hard sets per week), and taking sets close to [failure](./Training%20to%20failure.md). Research shows hypertrophy can happen with a wide range of [reps](./Rep.md) — roughly 6 to 30 — as long as the effort is high. The [pump](./Pump.md) and soreness ([DOMS](./DOMS.md)) are not reliable signs of growth.

Growth is slow: beginners might add around 0.5–1 kg (1–2 lb) of muscle a month at best, and the rate falls with experience. Hypertrophy and [strength](./Strength.md) are related but not the same thing.

_Avoid:_ "toning" — a muscle can only grow, shrink, or have more or less fat covering it. "Toned" usually means some muscle plus low body fat.

_Usage:_

"I've been training two months and I'm not huge yet."

"Hypertrophy takes time. Keep the sets hard, eat enough protein, and compare photos in six months, not six weeks."
