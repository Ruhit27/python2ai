description: Gym slang for a split that trains each muscle group on its own day, once a week — chest day, back day, arm day.

Slang for a [split](./Split.md) where each day focuses on one or two [muscle groups](./Muscle%20group.md), trained once a week: Monday chest, Tuesday back, Wednesday shoulders, Thursday arms, Friday legs. The name comes from how common it is in bodybuilding magazines and among gym regulars ("bros").

Its appeal is that each muscle gets a lot of [volume](./Volume.md) in one session and then a full week to recover. The downside is low [frequency](./Training%20frequency.md): research suggests training each muscle about twice a week tends to build slightly more muscle than once, for the same total weekly sets. Each session also includes a lot of "junk" sets once the muscle is tired.

It still works — many people build plenty of muscle on it — especially for experienced lifters who enjoy it. But for most people, a split that trains everything twice a week, like upper/lower or [push/pull/legs](./Push%20pull%20legs.md), is a better use of the same time.

_Avoid:_ "bro split" as an insult — it's a legitimate split, just not the most efficient one for most people.

_Usage:_

"Is Monday chest day, Tuesday back day a bad idea?"

"It's a bro split. It works, but hitting each muscle twice a week usually works a bit better."
