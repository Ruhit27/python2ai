description: How fast each part of a rep is performed, often written as four numbers, like 3-1-1-0.
---

The speed of each part of a [rep](./Rep.md). It's often written as four numbers — for example **3-1-1-0** — meaning 3 seconds to lower the weight, a 1-second pause at the bottom, 1 second to lift it, and no pause at the top.

The lowering part is called the *eccentric* phase; the lifting part is the *concentric* phase. Controlling the eccentric instead of letting the weight drop keeps tension on the muscle and helps [form](./Form.md). A pause at the bottom removes bounce, making the lift harder and more honest.

For muscle growth, research suggests tempo matters less than people think: anything from fast to moderately slow works, as long as the sets are hard and the [range of motion](./Range%20of%20motion.md) is full. Very slow reps — say, 10 seconds down — mostly just force you to use lighter weights. Tempo is a useful tool for learning technique, not a secret to growth.

_Avoid:_ "time under tension" as the goal in itself — it matters less than how close to failure your sets are.

_Usage:_

"Should I lift explosively or slowly?"

"Control it on the way down and lift with intent on the way up. A 2-0-1-0 tempo is a good default."
