---
description: General fitness for sustained or repeated hard effort — being in shape to work hard without gassing out.
---

Being in shape for sustained or repeated effort — the ability to keep working hard without getting exhausted. In gyms and sport, "conditioning" usually means training that improves this: circuits, sled pushes, carries, [HIIT](./HIIT.md), and other hard [cardio](./Cardio.md)-style work.

Good conditioning helps lifting too: you recover faster between [sets](./Set.md), hard workouts feel easier, and you can handle more [volume](./Volume.md). Many strength athletes add one or two conditioning sessions a week for this reason.

It differs from pure aerobic training like [Zone 2](./Zone%202.md) in that it often mixes strength and endurance and pushes toward the higher [heart rate zones](./Heart%20rate%20zones.md). Because it's tiring, it has to be balanced against lifting and [recovery](./Recovery.md).

_Avoid:_ "conditioning" as a synonym for cardio — it usually means harder, mixed work aimed at working capacity.

_Usage:_

"I'm wiped out after every set of squats."

"Your conditioning is holding you back. Add a short circuit twice a week."
