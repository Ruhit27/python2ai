description: The feeling of fullness and satisfaction after eating that keeps you from being hungry again soon.
---

The feeling of being full and satisfied after eating, which keeps hunger away until the next meal. Foods differ a lot in how filling they are for the same [calories](./Calorie.md), and high-satiety foods make it much easier to eat less without feeling deprived.

What makes food filling: [protein](./Protein.md) (the most satiating [macronutrient](./Macronutrient.md)), [fiber](./Fiber.md), water content and volume (low [calorie density](./Calorie%20density.md)), and foods that take time to chew. Boiled potatoes, eggs, oats, fish, beans, and vegetables rank high; pastries, sweets, and many [ultra-processed foods](./Ultra-processed%20food.md) rank low.

Satiety is the practical key to sticking with a [calorie deficit](./Calorie%20deficit.md). Sleep, stress, and eating quickly also affect it — eating slowly gives the body's fullness signals time to catch up.

_Avoid:_ "I just need more willpower" — choosing more filling foods makes hunger much easier to manage than willpower does.

_Usage:_

"I eat 1,800 calories and I'm starving all day."

"Shift toward more protein, vegetables, and potatoes — same calories, much more filling."
