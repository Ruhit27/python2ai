A [supplement](./Supplement.md), usually a powder mixed with water, taken 20–30 minutes before training to boost energy and performance. Most are built around [caffeine](./Caffeine.md) — often a large dose, 150–300 mg or more per scoop — plus other ingredients.

Common extras include **beta-alanine** (causes the tingly feeling; may help in sets lasting 1–4 minutes), **citrulline** (may improve blood flow and the "pump"), and sometimes [creatine](./Creatine.md). Many products use *proprietary blends* that hide how much of each ingredient they contain, making it hard to know what you're getting.

Most of the benefit usually comes from the caffeine, so a cup of coffee does much of the same job for far less money. High-stimulant products can cause jitters, a racing heart, and poor sleep if taken late. Pre-workouts are among the supplement types most often found to contain undeclared or banned ingredients, so third-party-tested products are safer.

_Avoid:_ "I need pre-workout to train" — it can help, but coffee or a good night's sleep does most of the same.

_Usage:_

"Which pre-workout should I buy?"

"Honestly, try a strong coffee first. That's the main active ingredient anyway."
