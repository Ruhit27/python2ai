The nine [amino acids](./Amino%20acid.md) the human body can't produce itself, so they must come from the diet. They include *leucine*, which plays a key role in switching on muscle building, as well as lysine, methionine, and others.

A protein source containing all nine in good amounts is sometimes called a *complete* protein. Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, and soy are complete. Many plant [proteins](./Protein.md) are low in one — grains in lysine, beans in methionine — but combining plant foods over the day covers it, so vegetarians and vegans can get everything they need.

Leucine content is one reason whey [protein](./Protein%20powder.md) is effective for muscle building. It's also the idea behind [BCAA](./BCAA.md) supplements — though without the other essential amino acids, leucine alone can't build muscle.

_Avoid:_ "you must combine proteins in every meal" — combining over the whole day is enough.

_Usage:_

"Is plant protein good enough for building muscle?"

"Yes — eat a variety and a bit more total protein, and you'll cover the essential amino acids."
