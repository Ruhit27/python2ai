---
description: The share of your body weight that's fat. A better measure of body composition than weight alone.
---

The percentage of your total body weight that is fat. Someone weighing 80 kg (175 lb) with 16 kg (35 lb) of fat has 20% body fat. The rest is [lean mass](./Lean%20mass.md): muscle, bone, organs, water.

It says more about health and appearance than weight or [BMI](./BMI.md), because two people of the same weight can look very different depending on how much of it is muscle. Healthy ranges differ by sex — women naturally carry more essential fat — and by age.

It's hard to measure accurately. DEXA scans are among the most accurate; smart scales (which use bioelectrical impedance) and skinfold calipers are convenient but can be off by several percentage points and are affected by [hydration](./Hydration.md). For tracking change, using the same method in the same conditions matters more than the exact number.

_Avoid:_ "my smart scale says 18.3%" as precise — home devices give rough estimates useful mainly for trends.

_Usage:_

"My weight is the same but my body fat dropped."

"That's recomposition — lost fat, gained muscle."
