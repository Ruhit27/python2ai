One of the three [macronutrients](./Macronutrient.md), providing about 9 kcal per gram — more than twice [protein](./Protein.md) or [carbohydrate](./Carbohydrate.md). Dietary fat is found in oils, butter, nuts, seeds, avocados, fatty fish, meat, and dairy.

The body needs fat to make hormones, build cell membranes, and absorb the fat-soluble [vitamins](./Vitamin.md) A, D, E, and K. Some fats are *essential* — the body can't make them — such as the omega-3s in oily fish, walnuts, and flaxseed.

Fats are grouped by chemical structure. **Unsaturated** fats (olive oil, nuts, fish) are generally linked to better heart health. **Saturated** fats (butter, fatty meat, coconut oil) are best kept moderate, according to most health guidelines. **Trans** fats, once common in processed foods, are harmful and now banned or limited in many countries. Because fat is so energy-dense, it raises the [calorie density](./Calorie%20density.md) of food quickly.

_Avoid:_ "eating fat makes you fat" — body fat comes from a calorie surplus, whatever its source.

_Usage:_

"Is olive oil healthy? It's all fat."

"It's mostly unsaturated fat, which is good for you — just calorie-dense, so measure it."
