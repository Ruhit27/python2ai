description: Non-exercise activity thermogenesis: calories burned by everyday movement that isn't exercise — walking, fidgeting, chores.

Stands for **N**on-**E**xercise **A**ctivity **T**hermogenesis: the [calories](./Calorie.md) burned by all the movement that isn't formal exercise — walking to the shops, taking the stairs, standing, cleaning, fidgeting.

NEAT varies more between people than any other part of [TDEE](./TDEE.md). Two people of the same size can differ by several hundred calories a day just from how much they move — a waiter might burn far more than an office worker, even if both go to the gym for an hour. It's often the hidden reason behind "fast" or "slow" [metabolism](./Metabolism.md).

NEAT also tends to drop unconsciously during a [calorie deficit](./Calorie%20deficit.md) — you move less when you're eating less — which is part of why fat loss slows. A daily step target is a simple way to keep it up.

_Avoid:_ "I work out, so I'm active" — an hour of exercise can be outweighed by sitting the other fifteen waking hours.

_Usage:_

"I train five days a week but can't lose weight."

"How many steps do you get outside the gym? If it's 3,000, that's your problem — NEAT."
