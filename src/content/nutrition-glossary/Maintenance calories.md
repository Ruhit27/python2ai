description: The number of calories that keeps your bodyweight stable — roughly equal to your TDEE.
---

The daily [calorie](./Calorie.md) intake at which your weight stays about the same over time — you're taking in roughly as much energy as you use, your [TDEE](./TDEE.md). Often called just *maintenance*.

Maintenance is the reference point for every goal. A [deficit](./Calorie%20deficit.md) is measured from it (for a [cut](./Cutting.md)), and so is a [surplus](./Calorie%20surplus.md) (for a [bulk](./Bulking.md)). [Recomposition](./Body%20recomposition.md) often happens around it.

It isn't fixed. It drops as you lose weight (a smaller body needs less), rises as you gain, and moves with activity — a new desk job or a new habit of walking can shift it by hundreds of calories. Daily weight also fluctuates with [water weight](./Water%20weight.md), so maintenance is best judged from weekly averages, not single weigh-ins.

_Avoid:_ "my maintenance is 2,000" as a permanent fact — it changes with your weight and activity.

_Usage:_

"I've lost 8 kg (18 lb) and stopped losing. Did my metabolism break?"

"No — your maintenance is lower now that you're lighter. Your old deficit is close to your new maintenance."
