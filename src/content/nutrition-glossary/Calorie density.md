How many [calories](./Calorie.md) a food provides per gram (or per 100 g). Foods high in water and [fiber](./Fiber.md) — vegetables, fruit, soups, lean proteins — are low in calorie density; foods high in [fat](./Fat.md) and low in water — oils, nuts, chocolate, chips — are high.

| Food | Approx. kcal per 100 g |
| --- | --- |
| Cucumber | 15 |
| Apple | 50 |
| Cooked rice | 130 |
| Cheddar cheese | 400 |
| Olive oil | 880 |

Because people tend to eat a fairly steady *volume* of food, choosing lower-density foods makes it easier to eat fewer calories while still feeling full ([satiety](./Satiety.md)). This is often called *volume eating* and is a useful strategy during a [cut](./Cutting.md). High-density foods are useful when you need more calories, such as during a [bulk](./Bulking.md).

_Avoid:_ "healthy food can't make you gain weight" — nuts and olive oil are healthy but very calorie-dense.

_Usage:_

"How do I eat less without being hungry?"

"Swap some calorie-dense foods for low-density ones — more vegetables, fruit, and soup."
