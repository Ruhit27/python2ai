The nutrients the body needs in large amounts, and which provide energy ([calories](./Calorie.md)): [protein](./Protein.md), [carbohydrate](./Carbohydrate.md), and [fat](./Fat.md). Often shortened to *macros*. (Alcohol also provides calories but isn't a nutrient the body needs.)

| Macro | Energy per gram | Main jobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protein | ~4 kcal | Building and repairing tissue |
| Carbohydrate | ~4 kcal | Main fuel for the brain and hard exercise |
| Fat | ~9 kcal | Energy storage, hormones, absorbing some vitamins |

Most foods contain a mix: chicken is mostly protein, rice mostly carbohydrate, olive oil almost pure fat. The balance of macros matters for goals like building muscle (enough protein) and for how full you feel ([satiety](./Satiety.md)), while total calories decide weight change.

Counting grams of each is called [tracking macros](./Tracking%20macros.md). The nutrients needed only in tiny amounts — vitamins and minerals — are [micronutrients](./Micronutrient.md).

_Avoid:_ "macros" to mean only protein — all three count, and so do their calories.

_Usage:_

"What are my macros?"

"How many grams of protein, carbs, and fat you eat. Right now you're low on protein and high on fat."
