---
description: A circumstance or actor with the potential to cause harm by exploiting a weakness or disrupting an asset.
---

Anything that could cause harm to a system or its data. A threat can be a person or group — a [threat actor](./Threat%20actor.md) like a criminal gang — or a circumstance: a flood, a power cut, or an employee accidentally deleting files.

A threat only causes harm if it meets a weakness. A burglar (threat) is only a problem if a door is unlocked ([vulnerability](./Vulnerability.md)). In security, the same logic applies: a gang sending [phishing](./Phishing.md) emails is a threat; staff who've never learned to spot them are a vulnerability. The combination, weighed by likelihood and impact, is [risk](./Risk.md).

When a threat actually does cause harm, or seriously threatens to, the result is a [security incident](./Security%20incident.md).

_Avoid:_ "threat", "vulnerability", and "risk" as synonyms — a threat is what could cause harm; a vulnerability is the weakness; risk combines them.

_Usage:_

"Is ransomware a vulnerability?"

"It's a threat. The vulnerability is whatever lets it in — say, a server that was never patched."
