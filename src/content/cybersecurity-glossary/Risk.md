description: The possibility of loss or harm, considered in terms of how likely a threat is to affect an asset and how serious the impact could be.
---

The chance that something bad happens, weighed by how bad it would be. In security, risk combines a [threat](./Threat.md), a [vulnerability](./Vulnerability.md) it could use, and the [asset](./Asset.md) that would be affected. It's often summarized as **likelihood × impact**.

A laptop with no password left in a busy café is high likelihood; if it holds the entire customer database, the impact is also high — a serious risk. The same laptop holding nothing but public brochures is a much smaller one.

Risk can be handled in four ways: **reduce** it (add [controls](./Security%20control.md)), **transfer** it (buy insurance), **avoid** it (stop doing the risky thing), or **accept** it (decide it's not worth fixing). No organization can remove all risk, so the goal is to understand it and choose deliberately. A [risk assessment](./Risk%20assessment.md) is the structured way to do that.

_Avoid:_ "zero risk" — the aim is acceptable risk, chosen on purpose, not no risk at all.

_Usage:_

"Should we fix everything the audit found?"

"Rank them by risk first. Some are unlikely and low-impact — we might just accept those."
